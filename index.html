About Fast Food Billing System

Our Mission

We empower quick-service restaurants with lightweight, modern billing solutions that streamline operations, reduce wait times, and enhance customer experience. Our cloud-ready POS system puts control in your hands.

Why Choose Us?

  • Zero Setup: No servers, no backend—everything runs locally in your browser.
  • Beautiful UI: Modern glassmorphism design with smooth animations.
  • Full Control: Manage inventory, orders, and customers in real-time.
  • Global Access: Light/dark theme, responsive design for any device.
  • Smart Billing: Automatic tax calculation, discounts, PDF receipts.

Built With Modern Tech

HTML5 • CSS3 • Vanilla JavaScript • LocalStorage • FontAwesome Icons • Google Fonts

No frameworks, no dependencies—pure web standards for maximum compatibility.
